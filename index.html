The correction that matters most

Free ≠ the whole report.

The First Read is free — it names your primary pattern. The full Pattern Report (all patterns, foundation flags, lab markers, the clinician brief) is the optional €35 next step. Never say "free report." Say: "your free First Read," then "the optional Pattern Report." And the Founding Circle email opt-in is separate from starting a reading — never gate the First Read behind a newsletter signup.

Three commercial paths

Discovery post → follow / share / profile visit.

Utility post → free appointment worksheet → email opt-in → Founding Circle welcome.

Product proof → free First Read → optional Pattern Report. Cohort invite stays separate.

Do not gate the First Read behind newsletter consent. The cohort is valuable because women deliberately choose to join.

The lead magnet

Build before launch

Title: Bring the Whole Picture

Promise: a one-page worksheet to organise what changed, when, what appears together, and what you want to ask.

Format: printable PDF + mobile web version.

Landing headline: "Your next appointment deserves the whole story."

Button: "Send me the worksheet."

Only promote it once it exists and the delivery is tested.

Consent & segmentation

Fields: email; optional first name; optional interest ("understanding patterns" / "preparing for an appointment" / "skin tone & health evidence").

Separate marketing-consent checkbox, unticked by default. Privacy link, unsubscribe, and a plain description of what you'll send.

Never collect symptom histories in email signup fields — health answers stay inside the protected product flow (GDPR Art. 9).

Segment by source (founder / pattern / worksheet / demo) with UTMs.

Production control room

Batch the founder films first, then pattern explainers, then screen recordings. Film all four weeks before the posting sprint if you can; leave room to react to audience language.

Camera day A · Founder

Film: the 4 Monday founder films (your conversational scripts).

Wardrobe: one hero look; alternate jacket/earrings per chapter.

Setup: eye-level lens, warm diffused key light, no mirror glare, mic test, 9:16, negative space for captions.

Out: 4 full films + 4 teasers + 8 story cutdowns.

Camera day B · Education

Film: the short founder-led explainers in the week cards.

Method: record two hook variants each; pause between beats; grab 3–5s silent reaction plates.

Out: A-roll masters, clean audio, alternate hooks, thumbnail stills.

Capture day C · Product

Capture: First Read intake, primary result, optional Pattern Report, clinician brief.

Safety: test data only; remove identifiers; only claim features that exist in the live build.

Out: 3 demo reels + 6 stories + screenshot library.

Design day D · Editorial

Design: 8 carousels, 4 founder covers, reel covers, source footers, the worksheet.

Palette: bone #F2ECE0, gold #C48820, forest #1F3A2C, terracotta #B7593E (sparing). Real/authentic skin. No coral/blush, no generic medical stock, no fake testimonials.

Export: 1080×1350 carousel, 1080×1920 reel/story; title in safe area.
